Open-Source Tools

Reproducible diffusion MRI processing pipelines

⚙️

QSIPrep

A BIDS-app for preprocessing of diffusion-weighted MRI data. Handles distortion correction, motion correction, eddy current correction, and more.

Read the docs →
🔬

QSIRecon

Reconstruction of diffusion MRI data. Supports tractography, connectivity matrices, and a wide range of scalar maps for downstream analyses.

Read the docs →
🧠

pyAFQ

Automated Fiber Quantification in Python. Delineates major white matter tracts and profiles tissue properties along their length.
